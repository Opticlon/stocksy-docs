Stocksy: Back in Stock helps you recover lost sales when products sell out. Shoppers subscribe from your storefront, and Stocksy emails them when items are back in stock, drop in price, or become available as coming soon.

## How it works

1. A customer tries to buy a product that is out of stock (or wants a price-drop / coming-soon alert).
2. They enter their email in the Stocksy widget on your storefront.
3. Stocksy tracks that subscriber against the product or variant.
4. When stock returns (or the alert condition is met), Stocksy sends a notification email.
5. The customer returns to your store to complete the purchase.

You manage everything from the Stocksy app inside your Shopify admin — no coding required.

## What you can do with Stocksy

| Feature | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Back in stock alerts** | Notify customers when a sold-out product is available again |
| **Price drop alerts** | Let shoppers subscribe to price-drop notifications |
| **Coming soon alerts** | Collect interest before a product launches (paid plans only) |
| **Storefront widgets** | Customize how signup forms look on your theme |
| **Email templates** | Control notification content and sender settings |
| **Dashboard analytics** | Track subscribers, notifications, products, and delivery health |
| **Plans & wallet** | Use a monthly email quota, then top up with Notification Wallet credits |

## Who is this app for?

Stocksy is built for Shopify merchants who want to:

- Recover demand from out-of-stock products
- Build waitlists for launches and restocks
- Automate email alerts without a complex marketing stack
- Monitor subscribers, delivery health, and email usage from one dashboard

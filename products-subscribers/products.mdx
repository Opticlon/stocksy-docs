The **Products** page shows every product Stocksy is tracking for waitlist and notification activity. Open it from the Stocksy app sidebar or from **View All Products** on the Home dashboard.

Use this page to compare product performance, find products with waiting subscribers, and open a product modal for full subscriber and report details.

## Summary cards

At the top of the page, four cards summarize your catalog activity:

| Metric | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Products Being Tracked** | How many products have subscriber or notification activity |
| **Total Subscribers** | All subscribers across tracked products |
| **Notifications Sent** | Total notifications sent for tracked products |
| **Waiting Subscribers** | Subscribers still waiting to be notified |

## Filters, sort, and refresh

Above the table you can:

- Filter the product list (default **All**)
- Sort products (for example, **Most Subscribers**)
- Click **Refresh** to reload the latest product stats

## Search products

Use **Search by product name** to find a tracked product quickly.

## Products table

The table lists all tracked products:

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Product** | Thumbnail, product name, and variant count |
| **Subscribers** | Total subscribers for that product |
| **Waiting** | Subscribers still waiting for a notification |
| **Notified** | Subscribers who have already been notified |
| **Notifications Sent** | How many notifications were sent for the product |
| **Last Activity** | When the product last had subscriber or notification activity |
| **Actions** | Click **View** to open the product details modal |

## View product details

Click **View** on any row to open that product’s modal. The modal shows product-level data without leaving the Products page.

### Product header

At the top of the modal you see:

- Product thumbnail and name
- **Tracked** status badge
- Product ID
- Summary cards for that product: **Subscribers**, **Waiting**, **Notified**, and **Emails Sent**

### Subscribers tab

The **Subscribers** tab lists everyone subscribed to this product:

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | Subscriber name |
| **Email** | Subscriber email |
| **Phone** | Subscriber phone (if collected) |
| **Variant** | Product variant they subscribed to |
| **Status** | Current state (for example, **Notified**) |
| **Subscribed Date** | When they subscribed |

### Reports tab

Switch to **Reports** for product-level notification reporting and activity for that product.

### Close the modal

Click **Close** or the **X** to return to the Products table.

<Tip>
  Use **View** when you need one product’s full subscriber list. Use the main [Subscribers](/products-subscribers/subscribers) page when you want to search or export across all products.
</Tip>

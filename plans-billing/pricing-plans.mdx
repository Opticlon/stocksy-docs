The **Plans** page is where you review your current plan, email usage, Notification Wallet, and available upgrades. Open it from the Stocksy app sidebar, **Upgrade Plan** / **View plans** on the dashboard, or **View plans** in email Advanced settings.

Choose the plan that fits your store. You can upgrade anytime as waitlist and notification volume grows.

## Current plan usage

The top card shows your active plan, monthly price, and email usage.

| Detail | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Plan name** | Your current plan (for example, Starter) |
| **Price** | Monthly subscription price |
| **Email notifications usage** | Emails used this month vs your plan quota (for example, `3/1000`) |
| **Remaining this month** | How many plan emails you still have |
| **Quota renews** | Date and time when the monthly quota resets to the full plan amount |

Wallet top-ups never renew or reset with the month. Unused wallet balance carries over.

## Notification wallet

The **Notification wallet** is used only after your monthly plan quota is finished.

| Detail | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Balance** | Current prepaid wallet amount |
| **Rate** | $1 per 100 emails after quota |
| **Top up** | Add more wallet funds |

Purchased wallet balance never resets on month renew. It only decreases when you send emails past your plan quota.

<Note>
  The Free plan has no wallet top-up. Sending stops when the free monthly quota is used.
</Note>

## Available plans

| Plan | Price | Notifications / month | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Free** | $0.00/mo | 100 | New stores testing alerts |
| **Starter** | $9.99/mo | 1,000 | Small stores getting started |
| **Growth** | $19.99/mo | 3,000 | Growing stores scaling waitlists |
| **Pro** | $49.99/mo | 10,000 | Established stores with higher volume |

### Free — $0.00/mo

- 100 notifications/mo
- Quota renews monthly
- No wallet top-up — sending stops when quota is used
- Email notifications only
- Back in stock and price drop widgets
- Stocksy branding on widgets
- Capped active subscribers

Coming soon waitlist is **not** included on Free.

### Starter — $9.99/mo

- 1,000 notifications/mo
- After quota: $1 per 100 emails
- Top-up balance never resets on renew
- Everything in Free, plus:
  - Customize button, sign-up forms, and email templates
  - Minimum stock limit
  - All integrations
  - 7x24 support

### Growth — $19.99/mo

- 3,000 notifications/mo
- After quota: $1 per 100 emails
- Top-up balance never resets on renew
- Everything in Starter, plus:
  - Remove Stocksy branding
  - Basic analytics
  - Higher subscriber cap
  - 7x24 support

### Pro — $49.99/mo

- 10,000 notifications/mo
- After quota: $1 per 100 emails
- Top-up balance never resets on renew
- Everything in Growth, plus:
  - Custom branded email domain (marked **Coming soon** in the app)
  - Unlimited subscribers
  - Full analytics and reports
  - Priority support

## Widget availability by plan

| Widget | Free | Starter | Growth | Pro |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Back in stock** | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Price drop alert** | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Coming soon waitlist** | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |

## Change your plan

On each plan card:

- **Current plan** — your active plan (button disabled)
- **Upgrade** — move to a higher plan
- **Downgrade** — move to a lower plan (for example, back to Free)

After you change plans, confirm the new quota on the current plan usage card.

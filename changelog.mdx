## Stocksy is ready

Stocksy: Back in Stock is live for Shopify merchants. Use waitlist widgets, email notifications, and dashboard tools to recover sales from sold-out and launch products.

### Features

- **Back in stock widget** — Let shoppers subscribe when products are sold out, with customizable button, form, and success message
- **Price drop alert widget** — Notify customers when a product price drops
- **Coming soon waitlist widget** — Collect launch interest on paid plans; choose products, show at inventory 0, hide when in stock
- **Email notifications** — Templates for all three alert types with content, colors, fonts, and style controls
- **Dashboard** — Setup guide, overview metrics, charts, top products, recent activity, system health, and plan usage
- **Products** — Tracked products list with View modal for subscribers and reports
- **Subscribers** — Filter by alert type, search, and export
- **Reports** — Track pending, processing, sent, and failed notifications with CSV export
- **Plans & Notification Wallet** — Free, Starter, Growth, and Pro plans with monthly email quota and wallet top-ups
- **Settings** — General, Branding, Preferences, Data, and About

### Improvements

- Theme App Embed status on the Home dashboard
- Live preview while customizing widgets and emails
- Shared store branding across all email templates
- Shared sending domain with Pro custom domain marked Coming soon
